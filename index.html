About Adimi

An artwork into an opportunity to learn

We build a practical tool for middle-school students to observe, experiment, make choices, understand outcomes, and develop their own way of thinking.

Students can benefit through Adimi with short art lessons, guided intention-setting, offline drawing, AI-assisted observation and learner reflection.

Our Positioning

Why Adimi

Built around learner agency

Student first, AI second

Before seeing the AI's observation, students first describe what they see in their own artwork.

No automatic judgment

Adimi does not tell students whether their artwork is good or bad.

Measurable observation

A student's image is measured through image processing and a 3×3 grid to explicitly describe or refuse the result rather than asking a language model to freely interpret their work.

Designed with boundaries

Adimi does not draw, grade, evaluate, compare, or infer personality or psychological states.

Our Solution

A complete learning loop - from intention to reflection

Adimi turns one drawing session into a structured learning experience.

01 · Learn

A short art lesson introduces one concept.

02 · Set an Intention

Students choose references, answer guiding questions and define what they want to explore.

03 · Draw

Students draw independently on paper. AI steps away from the creative process.

04 · Observe

Students photograph their work and describe what they see before receiving AI feedback.

05 · Decide

Students choose: Keep · Edit · Think Further.

06 · Reflect

Students reflect on the decision they have made.

One concept. One learning loop. Four sessions.

What We Are Learning

Can learners use feedback to make their own decisions?

Adimi's MVP is designed to test whether learners can actually use feedback to make their own decisions. Our pilot focuses on three questions:

Feasibility

Can students complete the full learning loop?

Agency

Can students choose to keep, edit or think further, and explain their decision in their own words?

Feedback boundaries

Can the system provide useful observations without turning feedback into grading, commands or unsupported interpretation?

Our Development Journey

From one concept to a scalable learning experience

Stage 01

Problem Discovery

Understanding how students respond to feedback and whether AI observation changes their decision-making.

Stage 02

Measurement Validation

Testing whether the visual measurement system remains stable across different drawing scenarios and common mobile devices.

Stage 03

Thin MVP

Building and piloting the complete learning loop with students learning independently at home.

Who We Serve

Making creative learning more accessible

Adimi brings self-guided art education to communities that lack resources, from underserved schools to families where parents, students, teachers, NGOs, Foundations, and CSR can benefit. It provides a space for children to experiment with art and develop their own creative thinking.
